How Agri-Chain Works

Bridging the gap between traditional farming and modern FinTech.

🌱

Tokenized Crops

Farmers list their crops as digital assets. Investors can buy fractional shares of a harvest, providing immediate liquidity to rural farms.

⛓️

Blockchain Verified

Every growth stage—from sowing to harvest—is recorded on an immutable ledger with geotagged photo proof for 100% transparency.

💰

Smart Settlements

Once harvested, investors choose between a physical delivery of the crop or a return on investment (ROI) via secure payment channels.
